# CRM Sales Analytics | Power BI Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project presents an interactive CRM Sales Analytics solution developed using Microsoft Power BI. It analyzes sales performance, revenue generation, sales pipeline efficiency, product performance, and customer account activity.

The project transforms CRM data into actionable business insights through five interactive dashboards, DAX measures, and a relational data model.

## Project Objectives

- Monitor overall sales performance and revenue.
- Analyze sales agents and regional performance.
- Evaluate sales pipeline stages and conversion rates.
- Identify high-performing products and customer accounts.
- Support data-driven sales and business decisions.

## Tools and Technologies

- **Microsoft Power BI Desktop** — Data visualization and dashboard development
- **Power Query** — Data preparation and transformation
- **DAX** — KPI calculations and business measures
- **Data Modeling** — Table relationships and analytical modeling
- **GitHub** — Project documentation and version control

## Dataset Description

The CRM dataset consists of four related tables:

| Table | Description |
|---|---|
| sales_pipeline | Sales opportunities, deal stages, values, agents, and dates |
| sales_teams | Sales agents, managers, and regional offices |
| products | Product information and sales prices |
| accounts | Customer accounts and industry information |

## Dashboard Overview

### 1. CRM Executive Overview

Provides a high-level view of revenue, opportunities, won and lost deals, win rate, monthly revenue trends, and product performance.

### 2. Sales Performance Analysis

Evaluates individual sales agents and regional offices using revenue, won deals, lost deals, and win-rate comparisons.

### 3. Sales Pipeline Analysis

Analyzes opportunities across Prospecting, Engaging, Won, and Lost stages to understand pipeline distribution and sales outcomes.

### 4. Product Performance Analysis

Compares product-level revenue, opportunities, won deals, lost deals, and win rates to identify high-performing products.

### 5. Customer & Account Analysis

Examines customer revenue, account-level won deals, industry distribution, and the relationship between customer revenue and successful deals.

## DAX Measures

Key measures developed include:

- Total Opportunities
- Total Revenue
- Won Deals
- Lost Deals
- Open Opportunities
- Win Rate
- Total Products
- Total Customer Accounts
- Accounts with Won Deals
- Average Revenue per Won Account

## Key Business Insights

- The CRM dataset contains 8,800 sales opportunities.
- Won deals generated total recorded revenue of 10,005,534.
- The win rate among closed deals is 63.15%.
- The GTX Pro product contributes the highest revenue.
- Sales revenue varies across agents and regional offices.
- Customer revenue is distributed across multiple industries.
- All 85 customer accounts have at least one won deal.

## Business Recommendations

1. Study the strategies used by high-performing sales agents and share effective practices across teams.
2. Prioritize products with strong revenue contributions while investigating lower-performing products.
3. Monitor open opportunities and improve follow-up processes to support conversions.
4. Use regional performance comparisons to guide sales coaching and resource allocation.
5. Develop targeted customer engagement strategies based on account and industry performance.
